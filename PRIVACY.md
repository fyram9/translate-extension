# Privacy Policy — AI Translate

_Last updated: 2026-08-22_

AI Translate does not collect, store, or share any personal data on its own.

**What happens when you translate:**
- When you select text and click **Translate selection**, that text along with your chosen target language is sent to the [OpenRouter API](https://openrouter.ai) (`https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/chat/completions`) using the API key and model you configured in the extension's Settings.
- The request is made directly from your browser to OpenRouter. The extension itself does not proxy or log the text.

**What is stored locally:**
- Your settings — target language, model ID, and OpenRouter API key — are stored locally in your browser via `browser.storage.local`. They are never transmitted anywhere except to OpenRouter as described above.

**What is not collected:**
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry.
- No data is sent to the extension developer.

**Third party:**
- Use of OpenRouter is subject to [OpenRouter's Privacy Policy](https://openrouter.ai/privacy) and the privacy policy of the underlying model provider you select. Review them before translating sensitive content.
